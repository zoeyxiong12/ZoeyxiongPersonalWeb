Product Designer

May 2025 – PresentLondon, UK

Research & discovery

I analyse competing products through hands-on use, compiling findings on user flows and feature mechanics into reports shared with the team. I also examine user survey results to understand users’ needs and preferences and inform future design decisions.

Feature & systems design

I’ve designed 70+ features across live products, turning product goals into user flows, interaction rules and specs for engineering and art teams. I also designed a reusable campaign framework for quick content and asset replacement, reducing engineering and art effort.

Implementation & delivery

I support feature configuration and review user journeys and art assets against requirements, providing clear feedback to engineering and art teams before release. I also designed an automated workflow to reduce repetitive production tasks and improve team efficiency.

Post-launch iteration

I review community feedback and product reviews, validating reported issues through hands-on testing. I also documented the user journey for two consecutive weeks after launch, combining both sources to develop actionable recommendations for future updates.
